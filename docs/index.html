Quick answer

Before Power Lines, power travels through a connected chain of touching powered buildings. A road or empty tile breaks direct contact.

What does your building say?

Pick the closest symptom. Check it in your game.

START WITH THE CONNECTION

Close is not the same as connected.

  1. Before Power Lines, check for a continuous chain of touching power buildings. An empty tile or road between them is a gap.
  2. For a simple early setup, place the Ore Smelter directly against the Burner Generator. Check the displayed connection in-game.
  3. Already have Power Lines? Check the route for forests or buildings blocking it. Lines unlock after the first Copper ingots.

CHECK THE GENERATOR FIRST

Fuel in. Soot out.

  1. Check the generator’s selected task and missing-input message. Early Wiki guidance lists Wood or Wood Waste; Hotfix #5 added a Planks-to-Power task and notes that some balance changes may apply only to new buildings. Use the tasks actually available on this building in your current build; do not assume every building has the new task.
  2. Check its Soot output space. A full Soot store can stop power generation; arrange storage that accepts it.
  3. If the generator resumes, check the consumer again. If it stays idle, inspect its own status before assuming the grid is broken.

TRACE ONE INPUT AT A TIME

Read the smelter’s own status.

  1. Check whether Power or ore is missing. The Ore Smelter needs both to make metal.
  2. If Power is missing, check the connected generator. If ore is missing, inspect the Mine and delivery route.
  3. If output space is full, inspect storage and its resource allocation. Do this before adding another smelter.

CHECK PATCHES AND CONSTRUCTION

A stalled move may be a different problem.

  1. Check your build and the move controls available in game. Hotfix #4 added cancellation of a Move Building order; Hotfix #6 fixed cancellation working only on Kewa’s Base Plate. Keep a save. If a cancel option is available and you want to undo the move, try it, then check the result in game. This does not guarantee that every stalled move is repaired.
  2. For unfinished construction, check that its required materials can reach the site. A paused supplier can hold construction up.
  3. If the problem persists on the current build, use the game’s F11 report tool. Keep a save; this page cannot diagnose a save-file bug.

Follow contact, not closeness

A useful early test is one generator touching one consumer. More elaborate layouts need their actual connections checked.

02 / FAQ

The questions behind “no power”.

Do Peeps carry electricity?

No. The official Power page describes automatic transfer between connected power buildings. Peeps move other resources.

Do I need to restart my world?

This guide has no evidence that restarting fixes your issue. Inspect the warning and current hotfixes first; use F11 for a persistent bug.

Why do the wiki pages sound different?

The general Power page describes a chain of touching buildings; the Burner Generator page describes the simpler direct-contact setup. We use the general rule and show direct contact as the first test, not as the only valid layout.

Is this connected to my game?
